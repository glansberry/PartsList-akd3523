BODY SERVICE PARTS LIST AKD 3524

Answers to Frequently Asked Questions

  1. I am using WindowsXP Service Pack 2 and Internet Explorer. When I open the CD, I get a warning message from Internet Explorer that reads, "To help protect your security, Internet Explorer has restricted this file from showing you active content that could access your computer. Click here for options..." What do I do?
        In WindowsXP Service Pack 2, Microsoft has implemented certain security features that block the running of code on your machine. To continue with the Parts List, you must turn off the warning in one of two ways:
    1. Temporarily turn off the warning for this session. Click on the warning text; a dialog box, labelled "Security Warning", should pop up. The message reads, "Allowing active content such as script and ActiveX controls can be useful, but active content might also harm your computer. Are you sure you want to let this file run active content?" Click the button labelled "Yes" and the Parts List should function normally. When you close Internet Explorer, the next time you run the Parts List, the same message will appear.
    2. Permanently turn off the warning. To permanently turn off the warning and allow active content to run, choose Tools/Internet Options from the Internet Explorer menu. Pick the "Advanced" tab. Scroll most of the way down the list until you get to the section labelled "Security". Check the options "Allow active content from CDs to run on My Computer" (if you are running the Parts List from the CD) and/or "Allow active content to run in files on My Computer" (if you are running the Parts List from the hard drive), and press "OK". Close Internet Explorer and open the Parts List from the CD. The warning message should no longer be displayed, and everything should work.

  2. I can't see the illustration image! Where did it go?
        Many of the Illustration images contain a lot of white space. Sometimes it is possible to start with an image that is too large to fit in the Illustration frame, and so all that is seen is the top corner of white space. If this occurs, you can reduce the initial size of all the images in the Configuration section. See Configuration.

        This situation can also occur when the Illustration image is 'zoomed in' too far. You can either reset the image by clicking on the words 'RESET ILLUSTRATION' in the Control frame, or you can reduce the size of the image by holding down the SHIFT Key and clicking on the image with the left mouse button, several times if necessary.

        Sometimes the Parts List/Illustration Frames can be resized to the point where the contents of the Illustration Frame are completely hidden. In this situation, you can resize the Parts List/Illustration Frames by either a) left-clicking and dragging the border between the two Frames so that the Illustration image is again visible, or b) in the Control Frame, click on the word ILLUS until the Illustration Frame becomes visible.

  3. The images are too large/too small when each Illustration page loads. Can I change the initial size of the images?
        Yes. Go into the Configuration Section by either clicking on the word 'CONFIG' in the Control Frame, or, navigating to Help>Configuration in the Menu Frame. Once there, in the Settings section, change the value of the number (expressed as a percentage) in the 'Sizing of Images on load' textbox. To increase the size of the images, increase this value; to decrease the size, decrease this value.

  4. I can see the Mechanical Service Parts List menu system, but where is the Body Service Parts List?
        Both Parts Lists, the Mechanical Service Parts List (AKD 3523) and the Body Service Parts List (AKD 3524) are accessible from the same menu system. At the bottom of the main menu of the Mechanical Parts List (loaded when you first start the Parts List) is a Section heading called 'Body Details'; clicking on this Section heading will load the main menu system for the Body Parts List. Similarly, at the bottom of the main menu of the Body Parts List is a Section heading called 'Mechanical Details'; clicking on this Section heading will load the main menu system for the Mechanical Parts List.

  5. When using the menu and clicking on an item that is near the bottom, the menu refreshes to a position with the control slide at the top, dropping the item clicked off the bottom. Is there a way to hold the control slide at its position, to assure the "current" line is shown?
        This behaviour has annoyed the developer too. The only cure that might help at the moment is to reduce the size of the fonts, which will cause the menu to take up less vertical space. However, this may not totally fix the problem for very long lists of sub-headings.

  6. If I print out the entire Parts List, will it look the same as the original?
        The short answer is 'No, but pretty close'.
        The long answer is that the original publication was typeset by hand by many people over a period of time. Thus the conventions that were used at the time of the publication changed through different publication revisions. Computers (and designers/programmers) like rules, not exceptions; when an exception occurs, it must be accounted for. These Parts Lists contain so many exceptions that sometimes it's hard to tell what was a rule and what was made up as they went along. Also, the Browser is singularly the worst tool possible when it comes to formatting for printing. To ensure that I don't go more insane than I am presently, I have tried to account for the majority of the exceptions, where possible.

  7. Can I update the Parts List?
        Yes! There are several different ways that the Parts List can be updated. Go to the Update section to learn more.

  8. I have found an error in the Parts List, what should I do?
        There are three kinds of errors: An error that is supposed to be there, an error that isn't supposed to be there and general errors that concern the look and functionality of the Parts List. Let's deal with the first two together.

        The hardcopy version of the Parts Lists contains errors, duplications and inconsistencies, all of which are faithfully reproduced in this electronic version. While transferring the Parts List to its electronic counterpart, I have come to realize that the creators of the original work had either a very finely honed sense of humour or a pact with the devil; either way, I am sure they cackled with maniacal laughter after every revision of the Parts Lists was produced. In any event, mine is not to question why, but to present it as it was.
        So, if you find what you consider to be an error, first check the Revision Number, or the Issue Number, of the page you are viewing. If there is no Revision Number, it means either that the page in question was never revised by BMC, or they didn't feel like including the number on the page. Compare the electronic version to the hardcopy version of the page with the same, or later, Revision Number. If the two are identical, then this is an error which was in the original hardcopy version and is supposed to be in the electronic version.
        If, however, the hardcopy and electronic versions do not agree, then there has been a transcription error or you have a page with a higher Revision Number than I possess. This is an error that is not supposed to be there. Please contact me with the details of the error: BMC Parts List number (e.g. 'AKD 3523'), specific information about the error and the following information from both the electronic and hardcopy versions of the pages: page number (e.g. 'MA 1'), and Revision, or Issue, Number (e.g. 'Issue 3' or 'Original').

        The third kind of error is general in nature, and could be anything from a scripting error or bug which interferes with the intended functionality or look of the Parts List, to a spelling mistake. For the former type of error, please contact me with the following information: Operating System Name and Version; Browser Name and Version; a detailed description of the error: include what the actual behaviour is, what the intended behaviour should be and any error messages that may appear; with the latter type, a friendly note would suffice!

        You can submit error/bug reports at LBCdigital.com.
