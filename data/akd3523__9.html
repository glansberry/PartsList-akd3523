MECHANICAL SERVICE PARTS LIST AKD 3523

Location of Unit Numbers

The Car Number (A) and the Body Number (B) are stamped on plates secured to the right-hand side of the dash panel. The Engine Number is stamped on a plate secured to the front left-hand side of the cylinder block.
The Overdrive Number is stamped on a plate secured to the top of the unit.
The Gearbox Number is stamped on the right-hand side of the gearbox casing. The Rear Axle Number is stamped on the rear left-hand side of the axle.
