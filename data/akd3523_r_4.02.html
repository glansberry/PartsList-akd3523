The Type HD Carburetter

  1. Slow-running valve.
  2. Fast-idle adjusting screw.
  3. Jet adjusting screw.
  1. Piston lifting pin.
  2. Piston/suction chamber.
  3. Throttle adjusting screw (when fitted).

Tuning

Single carburetters

1

  1. Run the engine up to normal running temperature.
  2. Switch off the engine.
  3. Unscrew the fast-idle adjusting screw (2) to clear the throttle stop with the throttle closed.
  4. Screw down the slow-running valve (1) onto its seating, then unscrew it 3-1/2 turns.

2

  1. Remove the piston/suction chamber unit.
  2. Turn the jet adjusting screw (3) until the jet (7) is flush with the bridge of the carburetter.

3

  1. Replace the piston/suction chamber unit.
  2. Check that the piston falls freely onto the bridge when the lifting pin (4) is released. If not, see items 15,16, and 17.
  3. Lower the jet by turning the jet adjusting screw (3) down 2-1/2 turns.

4

  1. Restart the engine and adjust the slow-running valve (1) to give the desired idling as indicated by the glow of the ignition warning light.
  2. Turn the jet adjusting screw (3), up to weaken or down to enrich, until the fastest idling speed consistent with even running is obtained.
  3. Re-adjust the slow-running valve (1), if necessary, to give correct idling.
