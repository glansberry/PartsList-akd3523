Search Tips:
  • Remove spaces when searching parts numbers
    • To search for part number AEC 1337 03, you would enter 'AEC133703' (minus the quotes) into the search textbox.
    • To search for part numbers AEC 3106 or AEC 3168, you would enter 'AEC3168 AEC3106' (minus the quotes) into the search textbox.
  • This was a British publication, so the spelling and terminology are distinctly British.
  • Terms may include the following characters: A-Z a-z 0-9 _ - . ' , $ %
  • The search is case insensitive.
  • Wildcards such as '*' and '?' are not supported at this time.
  • Add a '+' in front of terms that must appear in the results.
  • Add a '-' in front of terms that must not appear in results.
  • Common words (i.e. words appearing on more than 90% of the pages) are excluded from the search index.
