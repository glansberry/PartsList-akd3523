AUSTIN-HEALEY 3000 BJ7 AND BJ8

How to use this parts list

The left side is the contents. The upper pane is the parts table. This lower pane shows the plate, the exploded drawing for the page you have open.

  • Click a heading, such as Engine, then click a page underneath it.
  • The first page of a section is only a table of contents. It has no drawing. Open one of the pages under it to see the plate.
  • Body Details at the bottom of the menu opens the body list, AKD 3524. The rest of the menu is the mechanical list, AKD 3523.

Looking at a plate

  • Click the drawing to zoom in on that spot.
  • Shift-click zooms out. Ctrl-click recentres without zooming.
  • RESET ILLUSTRATION on the bottom bar returns the plate to its starting size.

Bottom bar

  • MENU, PARTS, and ILLUS show or hide those panes. Red means that pane is expanded.
  • SEARCH looks up a part number or a description. Leave spaces out of part numbers: AEC133703, not AEC 1337 03.
  • HELP opens the longer guide that came with the original list.
